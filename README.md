## Why this belongs in my engineering portfolio

The videos document my activity creating technical learning material and communicating engineering methods. Their subjects connect reaction engineering with spreadsheet automation, data fitting and numerical tools. They complement my professional experience in production, process control, logistics and EHS.

## Evidence and scope

The catalogue preserves the published titles. Topic groupings are editorial classifications based on the public listing; they are not a complete audit of the mathematics or teaching in every video. No learner count, formal accreditation, total teaching hours or industrial performance outcome is claimed. The videos remain hosted on YouTube. No original spreadsheet, source macro, notebook or textbook chapter is supplied in this repository.

## Development route

For future companion material, select a video and add the actual reviewed source file, assumptions, units, inputs, expected result and an independent numerical check. Distinguish any new recreated example from the original video source. See `COMPANION_TEMPLATE.md`.
